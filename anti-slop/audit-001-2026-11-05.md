## Numbered Findings List

### [01] R-02 (Copywriting): Em dash (`—`) characters present in UI copy and meta tags
- **Priority**: HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Violation**: The em dash (`—`) is used in page title, description metadata, hero banner alt text, and announcement ticker lines 6, 7, 8, 12, 249, 251, 258, 260, 310.
- **Rule**: R-02 strictly forbids em dash characters in agent-reviewed UI text.
- **Recommended Fix**: Replace `—` with clean separators: colon (`:`), middle dot (`·`), or hyphens (`-`).

---

### [02] R-24 & R-26 (Navigation & Interactive Elements): Dead footer links with `href="#"`
- **Priority**: HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Violation**: Footer social links (Instagram, Twitter, YouTube at lines 766–768) point to dead anchor targets (`href="#"`) while the top profile links already have valid destinations.
- **Rule**: R-24 & R-26 forbid buttons and links that navigate to nowhere or dead targets.
- **Recommended Fix**: Wire footer links to active URLs (`https://instagram.com/dhkaulia`, `https://x.com/dhkaulia`, `https://youtube.com/@jukiiakira`).

---

### [03] R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness): Social icons tap targets are below minimum 44px
- **Priority**: HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Violation**: Quick social row icon buttons are sized at `w-9 h-9` (36px × 36px), failing the required mobile tap target threshold.
- **Rule**: R-03 mandates minimum tap target of 44px for touch accessibility.
- **Recommended Fix**: Increase hit area to at least 44px (e.g. `w-11 h-11` or add padding) on mobile breakpoints.

---

### [04] R-26 & R-32 (Interactive Elements & Keyboard Accessibility): Non-functional monogram avatar with `cursor-pointer`
- **Priority**: HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Violation**: The profile monogram `JA` (`.avatar-monogram`) has `cursor-pointer` and hover scaling, implying an interactive control, but lacks click behavior, `tabindex`, or accessible button semantics.
- **Rule**: R-26 forbids elements that look interactive but do nothing; R-32 requires clear keyboard accessibility.
- **Recommended Fix**: Either make it smoothly scroll back to top on click (`href="#"` with top scroll) or remove `cursor-pointer` to treat it honestly as an identity mark.

---

### [05] Security / Protocol Hygiene: Insecure `http://` URL for TikTok links
- **Priority**: MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)
- **Violation**: TikTok links at lines 356 and 890 use insecure `http://tiktok.com/@jukiakiraa` instead of `https://`.
- **Rule**: Craftsmanship standard C-4 (Resilience & security hygiene).
- **Recommended Fix**: Upgrade to `https://www.tiktok.com/@jukiakiraa`.

---

### [06] C-4 Resilience: External visit counter displays unsightly "OFFLINE" flash
- **Priority**: LOW (Quality Lock)
- **Violation**: When third-party CountAPI fails or is blocked by privacy extensions / ad-blockers, the counter permanently shows `VIEWS: OFFLINE`.
- **Rule**: Craftsmanship standard C-4 (Resilience & graceful degradation).
- **Recommended Fix**: Cache last known count in `localStorage` or fall back to an active status indicator rather than raw "OFFLINE".

---

### [07] Frontend Architecture: Tailwind CDN runtime in production
- **Priority**: LOW (Quality Lock)
- **Violation**: Site loads `https://cdn.tailwindcss.com` runtime script in `<head>`, which Tailwind notes should not be used in production environments.
- **Rule**: Senior-Frontend Best Practices (bundle efficiency & rendering performance).
- **Recommended Fix**: Pre-compile CSS or extract Tailwind utility classes for instant loading.
